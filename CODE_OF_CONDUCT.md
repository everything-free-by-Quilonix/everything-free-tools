# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Everything.Free Tools to be a project people are glad they contributed to, regardless of experience, background, identity or circumstances. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and extend it before assuming otherwise
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Be clear about what you are certain of and what you are guessing
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct them, and move on
- Remember that contributors are volunteers

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, personal attacks or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Knowingly misrepresenting what a tool does with user data, or hiding data collection in a contribution

The last point is specific to this project: its tools are only trustworthy if their privacy labels are true, so deliberately undermining that is treated as a conduct issue, not only a bug.

## Scope

All project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, code review) and representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/everything-free-by-Quilonix/everything-free-tools/security/advisories/new), which is not publicly visible. Reports are reviewed promptly and the reporter's privacy respected. Maintainers may respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban.
